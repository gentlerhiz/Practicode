## Registration Process

### Step 1: Apply for the SIWES Program

To begin your registration, request the official SIWES registration form by sending a WhatsApp message or calling **09030578667**.

---

### Step 2: Complete Your Registration

Fill out the registration form carefully with accurate information and pay the discounted SIWES tuition fee of **₦40,000**.

Please ensure that all information provided is correct, as it will be used for your student records and communication throughout the program.

---

### Step 3: Confirmation & Onboarding

Once your registration form has been submitted and your payment has been confirmed, your admission into the SIWES program is complete.

You will then:

* Be officially enrolled as a Practicode Academy SIWES student.
recieve an acceptance letter from Practicode 
* Be added to the appropriate communication platforms.
* Receive your class schedule before resumption.
* Receive information about your instructors, learning materials, and other important announcements.

---

### Step 4: Resume for Training

Report physically to **Practicode Academy** on your scheduled resumption date to begin your industrial training.

Students are expected to arrive on time and come prepared for practical learning.

---

### Step 5: Pay the Administrative Fee

All SIWES students are required to pay a **₦10,000 Administrative Fee**.

This fee may be paid upon resumption if it was not paid earlier.

---

### Step 6: Begin Your Learning Journey

Attend your classes consistently, complete all practical exercises and assignments, participate actively, and work towards completing your Capstone Project during the three-month training.

Section 15
## SIWES Logbook Policy

All SIWES students are required to maintain and update their official SIWES logbook throughout the duration of the program.

* Record your daily or weekly activities accurately.
* Submit your logbook **every week** for review and signing by the Academy.
* The Academy will review your entries before endorsing them with the appropriate signature.
* Students who fail to submit their logbooks regularly may face delays in signing and may attract administrative penalties.

Keeping your logbook up to date is an important part of your SIWES requirements, so students are encouraged to treat it with the same seriousness as their classes and assignments.

Many students complete SIWES without acquiring meaningful technical experience because they are assigned routine office tasks with limited exposure to their field of study. At Practicode Academy, we believe your industrial training should be an opportunity to learn, build, and grow.

Our SIWES program is designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practice through hands-on training, practical exercises, assignments, and real-world projects.

During your three-month training, you will gain experience that helps you:

Apply classroom knowledge to real projects.
Develop practical, job-ready technical skills.

Improve your problem-solving and critical thinking abilities.
Work with modern tools and technologies used in the industry.
Learn software development best practices and professional workflows.
Gain confidence by building solutions from start to finish.

Whether you are studying Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity, Information Systems, or another technology-related discipline, a well-structured SIWES experience can significantly improve your career prospects.

At Practicode Academy, our goal is not simply to help you complete your industrial training requirement—we aim to equip you with practical knowledge, professional discipline, and the confidence to succeed in today's technology industry.

By the end of the program, you will have worked on practical assignments, completed a Capstone Project, maintained your SIWES logbook, collaborated with instructors and fellow students, and gained hands-on experience that extends far beyond the classroom.

Your SIWES should be more than just a requirement for graduation—it should be the foundation of your professional career. Practicode Academy is committed to making those three months one of the most valuable learning experiences of your academic journey.
