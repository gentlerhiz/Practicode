## Order Process

### Step 1: Browse Our Products

Explore our collection of laptops, accessories, customized merchandise, and other technology products. Review each product's specifications, features, condition, pricing, and available options before making your selection.

---

### Step 2: Place Your Order

Once you've decided on a product, contact us via WhatsApp or phone to place your order. Please provide:

* Product name
* Quantity
* Color, size, or specification (where applicable)
* Delivery location (if delivery is required)

Our team will confirm the product's availability and answer any questions you may have.

---

### Step 3: Make Payment

After your order has been confirmed, you'll receive our official payment details. Complete your payment and send your proof of payment to us for verification.

---

### Step 4: Order Confirmation

Once your payment has been verified, your order will be confirmed and prepared for dispatch or pickup. You will receive updates regarding the processing and estimated delivery or pickup time.

---

### Step 5: Receive Your Order

Choose the option that's most convenient for you:

* **Nationwide Delivery:** We'll deliver your order to your preferred address anywhere in Nigeria.
* **Store Pickup:** Visit our office to inspect and collect your order in person.

Our team will notify you as soon as your order is ready.
